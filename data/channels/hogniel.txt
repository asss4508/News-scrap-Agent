[2026-11-20 00:59] $V $MA $JPM - HACKERS LEAK CUSTOMER DATA FROM VISA, MASTERCARD AND JPMORGAN

Thousands of customers have reportedly been affected by a ransomware attack targeting third-party service providers linked to Visa, Mastercard and JPMorgan Chase.

Cybersecurity firm SOCRadar identified the ransomware group n0n as claiming responsibility.

Visa says no card numbers or payment data were compromised.

Mastercard confirms limited customer information was exposed but says its payment network remains unaffected.

JPMorgan says attackers may have accessed customer names, email addresses and marketing data.

All three firms say core services remain operational, with JPMorgan confirming the breach is unrelated to its earlier banking outage.
